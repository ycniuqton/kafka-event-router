Product fixes (each with a unit test)

Backlog drain stall. A backlog drained at ~185 ev/s per partition because lanes paused on every poll (high-water 512 < poll size 1000) and the re-fetch waited behind a 5 s idle long-poll. The fix sets FetchMaxWait to 500 ms and lane marks to 2048/1024 records with a 2/1 MiB byte cap. Test: TestRunnerDrainsBacklogBehindIdlePartition.

Lease expiry storm. Heartbeats were starved while Reconcile started hundreds of routes, so the leases (30 s TTL) expired and every runner restarted. The heartbeat now runs on its own goroutine. Test: TestHeartbeatsContinueDuringLongReconcile.
