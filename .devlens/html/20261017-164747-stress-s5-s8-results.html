S8: 20-min mini-soak at 45k

No leak: in every quiet window RSS was back at 85–90 MB and goroutines stayed at 71–78. After the run the worker was at 45 goroutines and a 16 MB live heap. 54.0M events with 0 lost and 0 duplicates. The p99 verdict failed because of two host I/O stalls (iowait 11–24 %, steal up to 15 %); between them lag was flat and p99 was 68 ms.
